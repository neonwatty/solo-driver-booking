# Change the return pickup time

The driver and rider agree to a new pickup after a flight delay.

1. In **Driver → Upcoming**, open the return leg and tap **Propose new time**. Propose moving the pickup from **7:15 p.m.** to **8:30 p.m.**
2. In **Rider → Manage trip**, show the confirmed pickup and the proposal side by side.
3. The rider taps **Accept new time**. Both views show the new confirmed pickup. The outbound leg is independent of the change.

**Demo behavior.** The original time stays confirmed while the proposal is pending. The example keeps the original fare.
