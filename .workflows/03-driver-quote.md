# Review a request and send a quote

The driver reviews the trip alongside their schedule and decides whether to take it.

1. In **Driver → Requests**, open the request and review the trip and upcoming pickups.
2. Enter a fare for each leg. The example starts at **$80** per leg. Tap **Approve and send quote**. The rider sees **Quote ready** and can review the price and pay.
3. Alternatively, tap **Decline**. The rider sees **Request declined** and can start a fresh request.

**Demo behavior.** The driver controls the quote. The rider’s trip is confirmed through the [payment workflow](04-payment-and-confirmation.md).
