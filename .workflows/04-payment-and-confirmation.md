# Pay and confirm the booking

The rider pays the operator after the driver approves the request.

1. Open **Quote ready**. Review the business name, both pickups, leg prices, the total, and the cancellation terms.
2. Tap **Pay $160** for the example quote. On successful simulated payment, both legs become **Confirmed** together.
3. The rider sees the receipt and **Manage trip**. Under **Driver → Upcoming**, each leg has its own schedule entry.

**Demo behavior.** All payment is simulated. A failed attempt follows [payment recovery](05-payment-recovery.md).
