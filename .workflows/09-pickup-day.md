# See pickup-day updates

The rider can see when to head to the agreed meeting point.

1. In **Driver → Upcoming**, open a confirmed leg and tap **En route**.
2. In **Rider → Manage trip**, show **Driver en route** alongside the meeting point.
3. The driver taps **Arrived**. The rider sees **Driver arrived** and the same meeting point.

**Demo behavior.** Updates appear in the booking when switching roles. The prototype simulates status updates in the browser.
