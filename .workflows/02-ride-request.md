# Request a ride

An invited rider requests a scheduled pickup with their driver.

1. Open the driver’s page after [accepting the invitation](01-client-invitation.md) and choose **One way** or **Round trip**. The demo defaults to a round trip from Cave Creek to Phoenix Sky Harbor.
2. Review or edit the route, outbound date and pickup time, and passenger and luggage counts. For a round trip, enter the return date, pickup time, flight number, and meeting preference.
3. Tap **Send request**. The rider sees **Awaiting driver approval** and the trip details. The request appears in the driver’s **Requests** list.

**Demo behavior.** The example uses an outbound pickup at 6:30 a.m. and a return pickup at 7:15 p.m. Passenger and luggage counts apply to both legs.
