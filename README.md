# Solo Driver Booking

## Product design draft

An invite-only, mobile-first booking website for independent drivers and their repeat clients. The driver brings the customer and runs the ride. The website gives both people one place to request, confirm, pay for, and manage a trip.

## Rider flow

1. **Accept an invitation.** The driver shares a private link or QR code. It opens that driver's booking page on the rider's phone. The rider verifies the invited email or phone number and continues in the browser.
2. **Request a ride.** The rider chooses a one-way or round trip and enters the pickup, destination, timing, passenger count, and luggage. For an airport pickup, they can include their flight and meeting preference. The trip shows as awaiting driver approval.
3. **Review and pay.** After the driver approves the request, the rider sees the driver's business name, the agreed price, and the cancellation terms. The rider pays that business. Payment confirms both legs of a round trip together and gives the rider a receipt and a link to manage the booking.
4. **Return to the same driver.** The trip link shows the confirmed pickup details and lets the rider request a change or book again. **My Drivers** shows the drivers whose invitations the rider has accepted. The first invitation opens the inviting driver's page directly.

## Driver flow

The driver invites clients and uses one dashboard to see new requests and upcoming pickups. They check their schedule, set the quote, and approve or decline the request. Their business receives the fare and handles trip changes, cancellations, and refunds. Each leg appears separately in the dashboard so the driver can manage a return without changing the completed outbound trip.

## First scenario

A repeat client requests a round trip from Cave Creek to Phoenix Sky Harbor. The driver approves both legs and sends the price. The rider pays on their phone and sees both pickups confirmed. If the return timing changes, the confirmed pickup stays in place until the driver and rider agree on a new time.
